I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Economics at the University of Utah](https://www.econ.utah.edu/).

I am an applied microeconomist by training, and my research examines how the design of environmental and energy policy — and the markets through which it is delivered — shapes who ultimately benefits from it. I am particularly interested in what happens when policy passes through intermediaries such as dealers and retailers, and in combining large administrative datasets with causal inference and experimental methods.
